Major Operations  
 SDRF  teams  attended  various  types  of Manmade  and Natural  disasters  and 
disasters  like situations  and cleared  uprooted  fallen  trees  all along  the Roads  & Hi-
ways  of Cyclone  hit areas,  restoration  of power  supply,  cleared  School  Buildings  and 
evacuated  the people  from  affected  villages  and shifted  to rehabilitation  centres  which  
resulted  in not giving  any scope  to loss of Govt . property  and public  lives .

River,  Nagavali  River) .

Konaseema , Eluru , West  Godavari,  Ananthapuram  & NTR  Districts) .
