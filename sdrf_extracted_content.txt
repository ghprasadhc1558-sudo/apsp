

Importance  of disaster  response  force : 
 The Andhra  Pradesh  State  Disaster  Response  Force  (APSDRF)  has 
indeed  played  a crucial  role in emergency  response  and disaster  management  in 
Andhra  Pradesh,  particularly  in coastal  areas . With  a 972 km coastline,  the state 
is prone  to natural  disasters  like cyclones,  floods,  Heavy  Rains  and 
earthquakes . The APSDRF's  efforts  have  been  instrumental  in saving  lives,  
conducting  large -scale  rescue  operations,  and mitigating  disaster  risks . •APSDRF  is a specialized  force  trained  for disaster  response  and relief  
operations . 
•Equipped  to handle  natural  and manmade  disasters  like floods,  cyclones,  
earthquakes,  Building  collapsed,  Train  Accidents  etc.  
•Trained  in rescue  operations,  first aid, and emergency  response .

•Rapid  response  to natural  and man -made  disasters : 
 SDRF  is rescue  Force  which  has rescued  people,  property  and live 
stocks  in every  part of the Andhra  Pradesh  State . It has reached  the remotest  
areas  of AP State . So far SDRF  has evacuated  and rescued  victims  during  
various  disasters . 
•APSDRF  primary  Goal  is to minimize  loss of life and property  during  
disasters  through  rapid  and effective  response . 
•Provide  immediate  relief  and conducts  swift  rescue  operations  during  
natural  and Manmade  disasters  and disaster  like situations . Objectives
