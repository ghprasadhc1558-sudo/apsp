Welcome to Andhra Pradesh Special Police

The Andhra Pradesh Special Police (APSP) is a specialized law enforcement force dedicated to maintaining internal security, disaster management, and public order in the state of Andhra Pradesh. Our force comprises highly trained personnel committed to serving the people with dedication and professionalism.

Established to handle special security situations, the APSP has evolved into a premier force with multiple battalions stationed across the state. We work in close coordination with state and central agencies to ensure the safety and security of our citizens.
